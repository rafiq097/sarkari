About Us
National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)
NESTS is an autonomous organization under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Govt. of India, dedicated to providing quality education to tribal children across the country. It manages Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) and CoE for Sports to promote holistic learning and development. NESTS aims to deliver modern education rooted in values, environmental awareness, physical fitness, and cultural preservation.
